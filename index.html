Section II: Legal Registration of the Association
1. Will the Asian Apicultural Association be registered as an international association in your country?
2. Will the Asian Apicultural Association be registered as a local association in your country?
3. Will the Association be registered under its official name, Asian Apicultural Association (AAA)?
4. Is there a government or official authority responsible for approving the registration of the Association and hosting its headquarters in your country?
5. Is there a government authority or official institution willing to support or sponsor the Asian Apicultural Association?
6. Do the laws and regulations of your country and the Association's bylaws allow the President of the Asian Apicultural Association to be from outside the host country?
7. Do the applicable regulations allow members of the Association's Board of Directors to be from different countries?
8. Do the applicable regulations allow individuals and institutions from different Asian countries to become members of the Association?
9. Do the applicable regulations allow the Association's registered headquarters to be relocated to another country if the members decide to do so in accordance with its bylaws?
10. If the Association's bylaws stipulate that the President cannot be elected from the country hosting the Association's headquarters, would you agree to this provision?
Section III: Headquarters, Administration, and Costs
1. Will official premises be provided for the Association in your country?
2. Will offices, equipment, secretarial services, and administrative support be provided to the Association?
3. Is there an organization willing to cover the costs of registering the Association and establishing its headquarters?
4. Will the Asian Apicultural Association be required to bear any costs related to registration, premises, or operations?
5. Will the Association's management and decisions remain under the authority of its elected Board of Directors without interference from the host organization?
6. Will the headquarters be able to provide administrative services to Association members across different Asian countries?
Section IV: Bank Account and Financial Management
1. Will an official bank account be opened in your country under the name of the Asian Apicultural Association (AAA)?
2. Will the bank account be permitted to receive membership fees from different Asian countries?
3. Will the bank account be permitted to receive international transfers and financial support from outside the country, in accordance with applicable regulations?
4. Will the bank account be permitted to transfer funds to other countries?
5. Will the President of the Association, even if residing outside the host country, be able to access the account and monitor balances and transactions electronically?
6. Can the President of the Association, or individuals authorized by the Board of Directors, be granted authority to approve withdrawals, transfers, and other banking transactions?
7. Can the authorized bank account signatories be changed following the election of a new Board of Directors?
8. Can the Association's funds be transferred to an official bank account in its name in another country if the Association decides to relocate its headquarters?
9. Are there any restrictions or special requirements relating to the bank account or international financial transfers?
Section V: Official Approvals and Supporting Documents
1. Can an official document be provided confirming that the Association can be legally registered in your country?
2. Can a preliminary approval letter or a letter of support be obtained from the relevant official authority?
3. Can confirmation be provided by a bank or relevant authority regarding the possibility of opening and managing the account and receiving and sending international transfers?
4. Are there any legal or regulatory restrictions that could affect the Association's independence or international activities?
5. Will the proposing organization commit to cooperating with the Association's Board of Directors to complete the registration and bank account opening procedures?
