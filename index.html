ASIAN APICULTURAL ASSOCIATION · AAA

Host Country Eligibility Questionnaire

Purpose: To evaluate legal, administrative, and banking arrangements proposed by prospective host countries, ensuring independence and international status.

Section I: General Information
Section II: Legal Registration
1. Will AAA be registered as an international association in your country?
2. Will AAA be registered as a local association in your country?
3. Will the Association be registered under its official name, Asian Apicultural Association (AAA)?
4. Is there a government or official authority responsible for approving registration and hosting headquarters?
5. Is there a government authority or official institution willing to support or sponsor AAA?
6. Do laws and bylaws allow the President of AAA to be from outside the host country?
7. Do regulations allow Board of Directors members to be from different countries?
8. Do regulations allow individuals and institutions from different Asian countries to become members?
9. Do regulations allow headquarters to be relocated to another country if members decide so?
10. If bylaws stipulate the President cannot be elected from the host country, would you agree?
Section III: Headquarters, Administration, and Costs
1. Will official premises be provided for the Association in your country?
2. Will offices, equipment, secretarial services, and administrative support be provided?
3. Is there an organization willing to cover registration and headquarters setup costs?
4. Will AAA be required to bear any costs related to registration, premises, or operations?
5. Will management and decisions remain under the elected Board without host interference?
6. Will headquarters provide administrative services to members across Asian countries?
Section IV: Bank Account and Financial Management
1. Will an official bank account be opened in your country under the name of AAA?
2. Will the account be permitted to receive membership fees from different Asian countries?
3. Will the account receive international transfers and financial support from outside?
4. Will the account be permitted to transfer funds to other countries?
5. Can the President electronically access the account and monitor balances/transactions?
6. Can the President or authorized individuals be granted authority for withdrawals/transfers?
7. Can authorized bank signatories be changed following new Board elections?
8. Can funds be transferred to another country if the Association relocates its headquarters?
9. Are there any restrictions or special requirements regarding the account or transfers?
Section V: Official Approvals and Documents
1. Can an official document confirm legal registration is possible in your country?
2. Can a preliminary approval or support letter be obtained from official authorities?
3. Can a bank confirmation be provided regarding opening/managing accounts and transfers?
4. Are there legal or regulatory restrictions affecting independence or international activities?
5. Will the proposing organization commit to cooperating to complete procedures?
Section VI: Additional Comments
Applicant's Declaration:
I declare that information provided is accurate and undertake to cooperate in supplying supporting documents.
